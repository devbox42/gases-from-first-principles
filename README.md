# Gases from First Principles

An animated, narrated study course for interested non-specialists through the research of Robert Hellmann (Helmut Schmidt University Hamburg): how gas properties such as pressure, viscosity and heat conduction are computed from quantum mechanics.

**Open the course:** https://devbox42.github.io/gases-from-first-principles/

Ten chapters, from pair potentials and quantum chemistry to virial coefficients, kinetic theory, gas thermometry and industrial mixtures, with interactive simulations and questions. Built from the published papers; simplifications are deliberate and marked on screen. Not affiliated with HSU or the author.

Single self-contained `index.html`; sources and build scripts are kept locally.
